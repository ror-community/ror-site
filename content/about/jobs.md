## Current openings

### Crossref Member Support Specialist (ROR focus)

Do you want to help improve research communications in all corners of the globe? Come and join the world of nonprofit open infrastructure and be part of improving the creation and sharing of knowledge as a **Member Support Specialist** at [Crossref](https://crossref.org).


* **Location**: Remote and global, to partially overlap with working hours in European time zones.
* **Type**: Full-time, Monday-Friday
* **Remuneration**: 47k USD or local equivalent. We pay salaries in the currency of the country in which you’re based. We arrive at the local USD-equivalent salary by determining the average 5-year USD exchange rate, to stabilize currency fluctuations.
* **Benefits**: Check out our[ Employee Handbook](https://www.crossref.org/operations-and-sustainability/working-at-crossref/) for more details on paid time off, unlimited sick time, paid parental and medical leaves, and more.
* **Reports to**: [Collin Knopp-Schwyn](https://www.crossref.org/people/collin-knopp-schwyn/), Member Experience Manager (Metadata Quality)
* **Timeline**: Advertise + interviews in November and offers in December


#### **About the role**

Crossref runs the global open infrastructure to link research objects, entities, and actions—creating a lasting and reusable scholarly record that underpins open science. Together with our >26,500 members in 171 countries, we drive metadata exchange and support 2.1 billion monthly API queries, facilitating global research communication for the benefit of society.

This role will initially focus on ROR curation. ROR (the Research Organization Registry) is jointly managed by DataCite, Crossref, and California Digital Library. It’s a community-led initiative to develop an open, sustainable, usable, and unique identifier for every research organization in the world. You can find out more about how DataCite, Crossref and California Digital Library all work together[ here](https://ror.org/about/).

The role combines detailed systems work with the human touch. You’ll triage and process community requests to add or update records in the Research Organization Registry (ROR), researching organisations carefully and making recommendations to the curation team.

You’ll start focused entirely on ROR curation. From 2027, as that work becomes shared across the membership team, you’ll also move into reviewing and processing Crossref membership applications and supporting Crossref members.

You’ll work with organisations across scholarly communications—journal publishers, research funders, museums, libraries, universities—so you’ll need an understanding of how research and publishing work, a systematic approach, and the confidence to ask probing questions when something doesn’t add up.


#### **Key responsibilities**

ROR curation requests:



* Triage new community requests for additions and updates using GitHub-based tools.
* Manage requests for additions and updates that are escalated by the contractors.
* Check the accuracy of the metadata by researching in ROR itself, organisational websites, and sources like Google Scholar, Wikidata, ISNI, and ORCID.
* Recommend to the ROR curation team and advisory board how each request should be handled.

Crossref membership applications, from late 2027:



* Review and process applications in Zendesk, following detailed process notes.
* Handle particularly tricky applications that are escalated by contract staff.
* Work with applicants to understand their organisational structure and the membership options open to them, brokering conversations between publishers, platforms, sponsors, and service providers where needed.
* Explain what membership involves: the community, the obligations, and why complete metadata matters.
* Run the administrative process end to end, including sending for sanctions checks, system setup, and issuing the DOI prefix and credentials.
* Keep our CRM (Sugar) clean and current, and work with the billing team so members understand what they owe and why.


#### **About you**

You follow a process precisely and you’re comfortable when there isn’t one. Our membership and our environment change quickly, and we won’t always have a clear answer, so some cases need slow careful work over email or Zoom while others need a fast decision.



* Extremely organised, with close attention to detail, in a role that gets busy.
* Able to research organisations and publications intelligently across multiple sources, and to make judgement calls against ROR’s inclusion criteria.
* Comfortable with spreadsheets and data analysis.
* Strong written English, and able to use open questions to get to the bottom of things when an applicant’s account doesn’t quite hang together.
* A genuinely global outlook—26,500 members in 171 countries, across every time zone.
* A quick study with new systems; we automate what we can and keep the human touch where it counts.
* Familiarity with GitHub issues and projects, and the ROR API, is useful (we can train you).
* Experience of Zendesk or a similar support tool, and a CRM like Sugar, is ideal.
* Knowledge of publishing, XML, metadata, or information science is a bonus. Professional English is essential; second languages are a strong advantage.


#### **About Crossref and the team**

We’re a non-profit membership organisation that exists to make scholarly communications better. We rally the community; tag and share metadata; run an open infrastructure; play with technology; and make tools and services—all to help put research in context.

We envision a rich and reusable open network of relationships connecting research organisations, people, things, and actions; a scholarly record that the global community can build on forever, for the benefit of society. We are working towards this vision of a ‘Research Nexus’ by demonstrating the value of richer and connected open metadata, incentivising people to meet best practices, while making it easier to do so. “We” means 26,500+ members from 170+ countries, 170+ million records, and nearly 2 billion monthly metadata queries from thousands of tools across the research ecosystem. We want to be a sustainable source of complete, open, and global scholarly metadata and relationships.

Take a look at our[ strategic agenda](https://www.crossref.org/strategy/) to see the planned work that aims to achieve the vision. The[ sustainability](https://www.crossref.org/operations-and-sustainability/) area aims to make transparent all the processes and procedures we follow to run the operation long-term, including our financials and our ongoing commitment to the Principles of Open Scholarly Infrastructure[ (POSI)](https://openscholarlyinfrastructure.org/). The[ governance](https://www.crossref.org/board-and-governance/) area describes our board and its role in community oversight.

It also takes a strong team – because reliable infrastructure needs committed people who contribute to and realise the vision, and thrive doing it. We are a distributed group of 60+ dedicated[ people](https://www.crossref.org/people/) who take our work seriously, but don’t take ourselves seriously - we like to play quizzes, measure coffee intake, and create 100s of custom slack emojis. We do this through fair policies and working practices, a balanced approach to resourcing, and accountability to each other.

We can offer the successful candidate a challenging and fun environment to work in. Together we are dedicated to our global mission and we are constantly adapting to ensure we get there. Take a look at our[ organisation chart](https://www.crossref.org/people/org-chart/), the latest[ Annual Meeting recordings](https://www.crossref.org/crossref-annual-meeting/), and our[ financial information](https://www.crossref.org/operations-and-sustainability/financials/).
